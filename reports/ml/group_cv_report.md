# Group-aware cross-validated evaluation

> **Preliminary development evaluation.** Pooled out-of-fold predictions over the 200-row audit corpus (23 discussion groups). Not final held-out performance: the models, taxonomy and thresholds were developed against these same rows. Generated by `python -m src.ml.group_cv`.

- Dataset hash `450745418479c703`; 5 group folds; seeds [42, 43, 44]; 1000 group-bootstrap resamples
- Design: Whole discussion groups per outer fold; calibration and Stage 1 threshold fitted on an inner group-held-out validation split of the training folds; group bootstrap over the reference seed's pooled OOF predictions.

## Reading notes

- Two taxonomy classes have no labelled rows; their metrics are undefined, and the e2e macro-F1 here averages only classes present in the corpus.
- 7 actionable classes share 60 actionable rows, so Stage 2 is learned from very few examples per class.
- 197/200 rows are YouTube; the Reddit slice is three rows and says nothing about Reddit performance.
- Labels come from one annotator; a first-pass/final kappa of about 0.30 (`reports/data_quality/dataset_audit.md`) means label noise is likely a ceiling on every metric here.

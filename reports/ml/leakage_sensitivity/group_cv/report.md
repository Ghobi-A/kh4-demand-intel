# Group-aware cross-validated evaluation

> **Preliminary development evaluation.** Pooled out-of-fold predictions over the 200-row audit corpus (22 discussion groups). Not final held-out performance: the models, taxonomy and thresholds were developed against these same rows. Generated by `python -m src.ml.group_cv`.

- Dataset hash `450745418479c703`; 5 group folds; seeds [42, 43, 44]; 200 group-bootstrap resamples
- Design: Whole discussion groups per outer fold; calibration and Stage 1 threshold fitted on an inner group-held-out validation split of the training folds; group bootstrap over the reference seed's pooled OOF predictions.
